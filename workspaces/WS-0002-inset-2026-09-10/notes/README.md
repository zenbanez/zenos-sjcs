# Notes

Observations about the day, and afterwards the aggregate of the reflection
sheets -- the counts, the tallies, planned versus actual, and themes drawn from
the written answers without attribution.

The sheets themselves never arrive here. They may carry names, and a teacher
who wrote something unflattering should be able to read whatever ends up in
this folder and not find themselves in it.
