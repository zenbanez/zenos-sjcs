# Admission Record — PPST Framework and Abridged TPD Booklets

## What arrived

Nine files landed in `inbox/` before this record: the Philippine Professional
Standards for Teachers (PPST) matrix, its adopting DepEd Order, and a
seven-part draft "Abridged Learning and Development System (LDS) for Teacher
Professional Development (TPD)" booklet series. All are DepEd reference and
policy documents — none contains individual learner, teacher, or school
identifying data.

## What was admitted

- `evidence/sources/2026-08-11-ppst-framework/`
  - `PPST _ with 2.5 _ 8June2021[518].docx` — PPST domain/strand/career-stage
    matrix, dated 2021-06-08.
  - `DO_s2017_042-1.pdf` — DepEd Order 42, s. 2017, the order that formally
    adopted the PPST nationally.
- `evidence/sources/2026-08-11-abridged-tpd-booklets/`
  - Seven draft booklets, Parts I–VII, covering Governance and Enabling
    Mechanisms (GEM), Learning and Development Needs Assessment (LDNA) plus
    its tools/templates annex, Planning, Program Designing, Resource Package
    Development, Delivery, and Quality Assurance, Monitoring and Evaluation
    (QAME). Dated July–September 2020, labeled "with New Normal."

## What was declined

One Microsoft Word lock artifact (`~$rt II_LDNA_Annex of Tools and
Templates_26 Sep 2020.docx`) was deleted as transport packaging per
[RFC-0005 §4a](../../constitution/RFC-0005-instantiation.md) — it carried no
content of its own, only a stale open-file marker.

## Why this is evidence, not yet knowledge

These documents are the operating guide for a real, ongoing program: DepEd
Tacloban personnel running the Abridged L&D System for TPD. Per steward zen,
the Learning and Development Needs Assessment (LDNA, Part II) data collection
is already complete outside this repository; the real next step is Planning
(Part III) onward. Admission here preserves the guide as citable source
material. It does not summarize, interpret, or promote any part of it to
`knowledge/` — that remains a distinct, later, human-reviewed step per
[RFC-0003](../../constitution/RFC-0003-knowing.md).

## Boundary note

Admitting these reference documents does not admit the underlying LDNA
results, individual teacher data, or any other personnel/learner/school data.
That data, if and when it is needed, is a separate admission decision for the
steward — see [INT-0002](../../intents/INT-0002-run-the-ld-system-for-tpd.md)
out-of-scope.
