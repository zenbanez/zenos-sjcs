# Returned work

Empty until 10 September.

Materials handed in by participants who chose to hand them in land here. This
is the folder opened on screen during the 3:15 block, which is the only reason
it exists.

## Naming

Files arrive named by the convention in the participants' own folder:

```text
Subject_Grade_Topic_FamilyName
```

For example `Math_G4_Fractions_Dela-Cruz`. Keep the names as they arrive. A
folder where a teacher can see at a glance that someone already made a Grade 4
fractions lesson is worth more than a tidy one.

## Arrival is not admission

A file being here means a teacher chose to hand it in. It does not mean the
work has been reviewed, that it is correct, that the house endorses it, or that
it has become institutional knowledge.

Anyone reading a file here should treat it as a colleague's draft, not as a
school-approved instrument.

Reviewing this material properly — deciding what is worth keeping, what needs
correcting, what could become a shared resource — is separate work under its
own delegation, and it has not been commissioned.

## What must not arrive here

- Anything containing learner names, parent names, addresses, or contact
  numbers. Check before adding, not after.
- The paper reflection sheets, or transcriptions of them. Those stay with the
  steward; only aggregates are recorded, in `../notes/`.
- Anything a participant asked not to be shared. Opting out is expected and
  costs nothing.

## Provenance

When adding files, record how each arrived — handed in at lunch, at the
afternoon break, or sent afterwards — in a plain list in this folder. The
distinction matters later: what reached the record on the day and what had to
be chased are different facts about how well the return path worked.
