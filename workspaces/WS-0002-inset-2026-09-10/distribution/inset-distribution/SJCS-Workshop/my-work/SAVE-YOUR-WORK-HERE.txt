===========================================================
  SAVE YOUR WORK HERE
===========================================================

Everything you make today goes in this folder.

Not in Downloads. Not on the Desktop. Here.


-----------------------------------------------------------
NAMING YOUR FILES
-----------------------------------------------------------

Use this pattern:

    Subject_Grade_Topic_WhatItIs_YourFamilyName

Examples:

    Math_G4_Fractions_DLL_Dela-Cruz
    Math_G4_Fractions_Quiz_Dela-Cruz
    English_G6_Persuasive-Writing_LessonPlan_Abad
    Science_G5_Water-Cycle_Worksheet_Villamor

The "what it is" part matters more than it looks. Without
it, the lesson log and the quiz you made for the same topic
end up with the same name, and saving the second one can
quietly replace the first.

Why it matters: at the end of the day these files go into
one place for the whole school. A folder of twenty files
called "lesson plan final v2" helps nobody. A folder where
you can see at a glance that someone already made a Grade 4
fractions lesson saves the next teacher an hour.

If you make several drafts of the SAME thing, keep the one
you would actually use and delete the rest.

Different outputs are not drafts of each other. A lesson
plan and a quiz on the same topic are two files and both
should stay.


-----------------------------------------------------------
CHECK IT ACTUALLY SAVED
-----------------------------------------------------------

Ask for the file to be saved into the my-work folder with
the name you chose. Then open the my-work folder yourself
and open the file.

Seeing the text in the chat window is not the same as
having a file. Take the ten seconds to confirm - it is a
miserable thing to discover at 4:00 that a morning's work
was never written anywhere.


-----------------------------------------------------------
WHAT COUNTS AS FINISHED
-----------------------------------------------------------

You have been through checking-the-work.txt, it contains no
learner or parent names, and you would be comfortable if a
colleague opened it next year and taught from it.

That is the standard. Not perfect. Usable by someone who
cannot ask you questions.


-----------------------------------------------------------
TWO COLLECTION POINTS TODAY
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Before lunch          - whatever is ready
  Before the 3:00 break - everything else

You will be told how to hand it in. It takes under a
minute and you do not need to do anything technical.

Handing in a file does not mean it is published or that
anyone will grade it. It means it stops living only on
your laptop.


-----------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU WOULD RATHER NOT SHARE SOMETHING
-----------------------------------------------------------

That is completely fine. Say so, or simply do not hand
that file in.

Nothing here is taken from you. This only works if what
goes in is what you chose to put in.


-----------------------------------------------------------
KEEP YOUR OWN COPY
-----------------------------------------------------------

This folder is yours. Take it home on your laptop.

Handing work in is not giving it away - you keep
everything you made.
