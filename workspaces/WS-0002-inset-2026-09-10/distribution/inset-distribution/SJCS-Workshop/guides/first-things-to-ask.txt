===========================================================
  FIRST THINGS TO ASK
===========================================================

Copy any of these, paste it into Codex, and change the
parts in [brackets] to match your class.

Start with the first one. It is the one you will use
tomorrow.

Do not worry about wording it perfectly. If the answer is
not what you wanted, just say so in plain words - "make it
shorter", "this is too hard for my learners", "we do not
have that material" - and ask again. That back-and-forth
is normal. It is not you doing it wrong.


-----------------------------------------------------------
1. TOMORROW'S DAILY LESSON LOG
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Using the templates and curriculum guides in the
  references folder, prepare a Daily Lesson Log for
  [Grade __] [Subject] on the topic [topic].

  Follow our school's template exactly. Our class period
  is [__] minutes.


-----------------------------------------------------------
2. A FULL LESSON PLAN
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Using the references folder, write a lesson plan for
  [Grade __] [Subject] on [topic].

  Include the objectives, the materials we will need, the
  activities in order with timing, and how I will check
  whether they understood it.

  Assume a class of [__] learners in a room with
  [describe what you actually have - blackboard only, one
  TV, no projector, etc.].


-----------------------------------------------------------
3. ACTIVITIES AND WORKSHEETS
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Make three activities for [Grade __] [Subject] on
  [topic]. One for learners who are struggling, one for
  the middle of the class, and one for learners who
  finish early.

  Use materials that cost nothing or almost nothing.


-----------------------------------------------------------
4. A QUIZ OR ASSESSMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Write a [10]-item quiz for [Grade __] [Subject] on
  [topic], matched to the competencies in the curriculum
  guide in the references folder.

  Include the answer key. Mark which items are easy,
  average, and difficult.


-----------------------------------------------------------
5. A RUBRIC
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Make a scoring rubric for [describe the task - a group
  presentation, a written output, a performance task].

  Use four levels. Write the descriptions in language a
  Grade [__] learner could understand if I showed it to
  them.


-----------------------------------------------------------
6. WHEN YOU ARE STUCK ON SOMETHING REAL
-----------------------------------------------------------

  I have a section where [describe the actual problem -
  most of them cannot read the textbook, they finish
  everything in ten minutes, they will not participate].

  Describe the situation without naming anyone.

  Give me three different approaches I could try for
  [topic], and tell me what each one would cost me in
  preparation time.


-----------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU WANT TO GO FURTHER
-----------------------------------------------------------

  Reading materials at a specific level:

  Rewrite this passage so a struggling Grade [__] reader
  can follow it, without making it babyish.
  [paste the passage]


  A letter to parents:

  Draft a short letter to parents about [topic] in
  [English / Filipino / Waray]. Warm, respectful, one
  page at most.


  Slides:

  Turn this lesson plan into a simple slide outline -
  title and three or four bullets per slide, no more.


-----------------------------------------------------------
ABOUT REAL SITUATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------

Several of these ask you to describe your actual class.
Do that - the answers are far better for it.

But describe people without naming them. No learner names,
no parent names, no addresses, no contact numbers. "A
learner who has missed a lot of school" carries everything
the AI needs; the child's name carries nothing it can use.


-----------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU SAVE ANYTHING
-----------------------------------------------------------
