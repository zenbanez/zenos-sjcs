===========================================================
  REFERENCES - OUR SCHOOL'S OWN MATERIALS
===========================================================

  NOTE TO THE FACILITATOR: this folder is EMPTY and must
  be filled before the USB sticks are copied. See the
  checklist at the bottom of this page.

  The rest of this note is written for participants.

===========================================================


This folder holds our own documents - the curriculum
guides, the templates we are required to use, and the
school materials your work should match.

The AI reads these.

That is the whole point of working this way. Ask a general
AI for a lesson plan and you get something generic,
written for no school in particular, in a format nobody
here uses. Ask it here, with these documents in front of
it, and you get something shaped by what we actually
teach and the forms we actually fill out.

You will need to look at these when you check your work -
the first of the seven checks asks whether your lesson
matches the competency in the curriculum guide, and that
means opening it.

But you do not have to read them before you start. To use
them, just mention them:

    "using the templates in the references folder..."

    "matched to the competencies in the curriculum guide..."


-----------------------------------------------------------
PLEASE DO NOT CHANGE THESE FILES
-----------------------------------------------------------

Everyone is working from the same copies today. If you
edit one, your work and your colleague's stop matching.

Save your own work in the my-work folder instead.


===========================================================
  FACILITATOR CHECKLIST - BEFORE COPYING THE USB STICKS
===========================================================

Fill this folder with the materials the day depends on.
Suggested, based on what the room will actually be asked
to produce between 10:30 and 2:30:

  [ ] The DLL template teachers are required to use
  [ ] The lesson plan format expected at this school
  [ ] Curriculum guides for the learning areas present
      in the room (check who is attending)
  [ ] Any assessment or rubric templates already in use
  [ ] The school calendar for the current quarter

Two cautions worth the thirty seconds:

  1. Check every document for learner names, parent
     names, addresses, or contact numbers before it goes
     onto thirty USB sticks. Blank templates are safe.
     Filled-in samples often are not.

  2. Prefer plain formats - .txt, .md, .docx, .csv. A
     scanned PDF of a form is an image, and the AI reads
     it poorly or not at all. If the only copy you have
     is a scan, retype the blank fields into a plain
     document. It is worth the effort; a template the AI
     cannot read is a template the room cannot use.

Delete this checklist section before copying, or leave
it - participants will simply skip it.
