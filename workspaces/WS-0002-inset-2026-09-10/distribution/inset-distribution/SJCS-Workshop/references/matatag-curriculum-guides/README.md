# Official MATATAG Curriculum Guides, K–6

## What this is

The 13 official MATATAG Curriculum Guide PDFs covering every core learning
area for Kindergarten through Grade 6 — the curriculum band this INSET's
attendees actually teach under. Fetched at the steward's direction on
2026-09-08.

**Why MATATAG and not the older K-12 curriculum guides:** the MATATAG
Curriculum rolled out by grade cohort — K, 1, 4, 7 in SY2024-2025; 2, 3, 5, 8
in SY2025-2026; 6, 9, 10 in SY2026-2027. By the 2026-09-10 event date, every
grade from K through 6 is under MATATAG. The older K-12 curriculum guides are
no longer the operative documents for this room.

**Language and Reading-and-Literacy are Grade-1-only by design**, not a
missing-file gap — MATATAG folds those into full Filipino and English
subjects starting Grade 2. The multi-grade-titled files (e.g. "Grades 1–10")
are DepEd's own authored scope; classroom rollout is phased by school year,
but the guides themselves were written for full vertical articulation.

## Provenance

Located via DepEd's own Phase-1 rollout page
(`deped.gov.ph/matatagcurriculumk147/`), which links to a `tinyurl.com`
shortlink DepEd itself circulates for the full MATATAG Curriculum Guide set.
That shortlink resolves to an official SharePoint folder owned by
`ouct_deped_gov_ph` — the Office of the Undersecretary for Curriculum and
Teaching — titled "MATATAG Curriculum Guides." All 13 files were fetched
directly from that folder: byte-for-byte size matches to the listing, valid
PDF structure, extractable text (not scanned images), and official DepEd
letterhead on page 1 of each. No third-party aggregator site was used as the
source of any file's content — only, where needed, to help locate the
official pointer.

## What was not independently confirmed

An automated search for the literal string "Grade 6" inside
`G2-10_Filipino_MATATAG-CG.pdf`, `G1-10_GMRC-VE_MATATAG-CG.pdf`, and
`G4-10_AralingPanlipunan_MATATAG-CG.pdf` did not return a hit, most likely a
table-extraction artifact rather than a real coverage gap — the titles and
page counts strongly indicate full-range content. **Worth a manual page-flip
before these are relied on for Grade 6 specifically.**

## What this still does not close

This is curriculum content — what to teach. It is not:

- the Daily Lesson Log / Detailed Lesson Plan **template** (tracked
  separately, see `../dll-dlp-template/` once added);
- SJCS's own assessment or rubric templates;
- the school calendar for the current quarter; or
- confirmation of any school-specific lesson-plan-format variation — a
  search of both DepEd Region VIII's regional memoranda and SDO Tacloban
  City's own memo repository (which currently lists zero uploaded memos)
  turned up no Division-specific override.

D-002 remains open for these.
