===========================================================
  REFERENCES - THE DOCUMENTS YOUR WORK SHOULD MATCH
===========================================================


This folder holds the official documents your lessons are
supposed to line up with. They are already here. You do
not have to find or add anything.

The AI reads these.

That is the whole point of working this way. Ask a general
AI for a lesson plan and you get something generic,
written for no school in particular, in a format nobody
here uses. Ask it here, with these documents in front of
it, and you get something shaped by what we actually
teach and the forms we actually fill out.


-----------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS HERE
-----------------------------------------------------------

  matatag-curriculum-guides/

     The official MATATAG Curriculum Guides covering
     Kindergarten through Grade 6 - every core learning
     area. These are the competencies your lesson has to
     match. Open the one for your learning area.

  dll-dlp-template/

     The official Daily Lesson Log and Detailed Lesson
     Plan templates, from DepEd Order 42, s. 2016.

     Use "dll-dlp-template.md" when you ask the AI for a
     lesson in the DLL format. The PDF beside it is the
     signed original, but it is a scan - a picture of a
     document - and the AI cannot read it. The .md file
     is the same forms, retyped so it can.

  context-from-zenos-deped/

     Background: the PPST framework and the Division's
     professional-development booklets. Useful if you
     want a lesson tied to a PPST indicator. Not needed
     for ordinary lesson work - skip it unless you have
     a reason.


-----------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE THEM
-----------------------------------------------------------

You do not have to read them before you start. To use
them, just mention them:

    "using the DLL template in the references folder..."

    "matched to the competencies in the Grade 3 Science
     curriculum guide..."

You will need to open one yourself when you check your
work - the first of the seven checks asks whether your
lesson matches the competency in the curriculum guide,
and that means looking at the guide, not at what the AI
says about it.

If the AI seems to be guessing rather than quoting, say
so plainly: "quote the exact competency you used, and
say which page it came from."


-----------------------------------------------------------
IF A FILE IS TOO BIG FOR THE AI
-----------------------------------------------------------

Some guides are long - English runs to 255 pages. If the
AI says the file is too large, or answers about the wrong
grade, narrow the question:

    "read only the Grade 4 section of the Science
     curriculum guide"

Tell the facilitator if this happens. A shorter,
grade-only copy can be made on the spot.


-----------------------------------------------------------
PLEASE DO NOT CHANGE THESE FILES
-----------------------------------------------------------

Everyone is working from the same copies today. If you
edit one, your work and your colleague's stop matching.

Save your own work in the my-work folder instead.
