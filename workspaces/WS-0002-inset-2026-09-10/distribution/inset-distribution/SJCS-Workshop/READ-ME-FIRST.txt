===========================================================
  INSTITUTIONS OF THOUGHT - AI IN EDUCATION
  San Jose Central School INSET
===========================================================

Welcome. This folder is yours for the day.

Everything you make today goes in here. At the end of the
day, the work in this folder becomes something the whole
school can use - not just you.

You do not need to read all of this now. Three steps.


-----------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - COPY THIS FOLDER TO YOUR LAPTOP
-----------------------------------------------------------

Do not work from the USB stick.

Copy the whole folder to your Desktop first. Then remove
the USB and pass it on if someone is waiting.

Working from a USB is slow, and if you pull it out by
accident you can lose what you were writing.


-----------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - OPEN CODEX AND POINT IT AT THIS FOLDER
-----------------------------------------------------------

Open the Codex desktop app you installed before today and
sign in.

Then open the SJCS-Workshop folder you just copied to your
Desktop, as your project or working folder.

If you are not sure how, raise your hand. Someone will come
to you. This is the one step where asking for help is
faster than figuring it out alone, and nobody is behind.

If you have not signed in before today, do that first. It
is the step most likely to hold you up, and it is much
quicker with help than without.


-----------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - ASK YOUR FIRST QUESTION
-----------------------------------------------------------

Open the "guides" folder and look at:

    first-things-to-ask.txt

It has questions you can copy and paste to get started.
Begin with the one about tomorrow's lesson.


-----------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
-----------------------------------------------------------

  references
      Our school's materials - curriculum guides,
      templates, and other documents. The AI reads these
      so your work matches what we actually use, instead
      of something generic from the internet.

  my-work
      Where you save what you make. There is a note inside
      about naming your files.

  guides
      first-things-to-ask.txt   - questions to start with
      checking-the-work.txt     - how to judge what you get


-----------------------------------------------------------
ONE RULE ABOUT WHAT YOU TYPE
-----------------------------------------------------------

Do not type a learner's name, a parent's name, an address,
or a contact number into the AI.

You will often want to describe a real situation - a child
who is struggling, a family going through something hard.
Describe it without naming anyone:

    "a learner who has missed a lot of school recently"
    "a section that is behind in reading"

You can hold the real name in your head. The lesson still
works. Nothing you know about a child needs to be typed
anywhere today.

Before you hand a file in, read it once more and take out
anything that identifies a particular learner.


-----------------------------------------------------------
THE ONE THING WORTH REMEMBERING
-----------------------------------------------------------

The AI does not know your learners.

It has never met the child in the third row who reads
two levels below the rest, or the section that is sharp
in the morning and gone by 2pm. It does not know which
materials your school actually has.

You know all of that. That is why you are still the one
deciding.

The AI is here to save you the typing, not the thinking.


-----------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------------------------------------------

If the AI stops responding or says you have reached a
limit, raise your hand. Do not restart anything or
uninstall anything. There are other ways to keep working
and someone will get you moving again.

Nothing you have saved in my-work is lost when this
happens.
