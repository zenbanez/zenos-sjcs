# WS-0003: Delegations

## D-001 — The open standing delegation (every registered participant)

- **Granted by:** zen, 2026-09-05, on minting INT-0003.
- **Holder:** anyone whose participant record and author-map row have been
  merged into `main`. It is not held by a named person and does not need to be
  re-issued for each arrival.
- **Standing:** Contributor (RFC-0006 §5, tier 2). Registration is the
  delegation event.

### What you may do

- Read anything in this repository.
- Draft lesson materials, assessments, notes, and other work products.
- Propose new records — intents, workspaces, notes — and amend your own.
- Review another participant's branch and say plainly what you think.
- Open pull requests, and respond to review on them.
- Disagree with the steward in writing, in the record, without asking
  permission first.

That last one is deliberate. A house where only the steward may object learns
nothing.

### What is not covered

Not because you are not trusted, but because these are the acts that are hard
to undo or that speak for other people:

- **Personal data.** No learner, parent, or staff names, addresses, or contact
  details anywhere in this repository.
- **Access.** Do not change repository visibility, invite a collaborator, or
  alter anyone's standing. The invitation is the steward's gate.
- **Voice.** Do not publish, share externally, or say anything as San Jose
  Central School or DepEd Tacloban.
- **Other houses.** Do not read from or write to any other ZenOS instance from
  here.
- **Merging your own work to `main`.** Nothing technically prevents it —
  branch protection is unavailable on this plan — so this is a request, and
  the reason is that a change nobody reviewed is a change nobody else can
  learn from.
- **Constitutional records.** `constitution/` is not amended here.

If something you want to do is not on either list, it is almost certainly
allowed. Ask if you would rather be sure.

### Review conditions

- The delegation continues for as long as the participant is registered.
- It is revised, not revoked, if lived use shows the bounds are wrong.
- Revocation of an individual follows RFC-0006 §10: evidence, dialogue, a
  decision record with the participant's response, then the warrant. Not a
  quiet removal.

### Note for a later reader

This delegation is deliberately wide. The steward chose an open posture on
2026-09-05, reasoning that this house may become the first with more than one
steward, and that friction added now costs more than rules added later.

Subok argued for a narrower opening — registration without repository write —
on the grounds that no ruleset is available on this plan, so any collaborator
can push to `main` directly. The steward considered that and decided
otherwise. The risk is recorded in
[INT-0003](../../intents/INT-0003-register-incoming-participants.md), and the
review conditions there name what would make it real.

Recorded so that if this is ever narrowed, it is narrowed for something that
happened rather than something that was feared.
