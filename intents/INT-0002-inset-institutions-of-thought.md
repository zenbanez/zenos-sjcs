# INT-0002: Institutions of Thought — the first INSET

## Purpose

Hold the record of the one-day INSET at San Jose Central School on
**10 September 2026**, and give the materials produced there somewhere to live
that is not thirty separate laptops.

The workshop's own thesis is that a school can become a learning institution —
that good work should outlive the person who made it. This house is where that
claim either becomes true or does not.

## What this house is for on the day

The facilitator's screen, and the record afterwards. **Not** the participants'
screens: teachers work from a plain folder copied off a USB stick, with no Git,
no accounts, and no invitations. That folder is a distribution, not this
repository.

## In scope

- The event's own record: this intent, its workspace, and its delegations.
- The school's reference materials, once the steward supplies them.
- Materials returned by participants who chose to hand them in, held so a
  colleague could open one next year and teach from it.
- Observations about how the day actually went.

## Out of scope

- **Registering teachers as participants.** Attending a workshop is not
  arrival. Nobody is added as a collaborator to run this event.
- Personal data about learners, parents, or staff. Blank templates are safe;
  filled-in samples usually are not.
- Treating anything returned on the day as evidence, adopted knowledge, or an
  institutional decision. Arrival is below admission, and admission is a
  separate steward judgment.
- Any claim about the quality of participant work that the work itself has not
  been reviewed to support.
- Speaking for San Jose Central School, DepEd Tacloban, or any division office.
- Publication, external sharing, or making this repository public.

## Success conditions

1. Every teacher who wants to leaves with something usable the next day.
2. What was handed in is legible to someone who cannot ask its author a
   question.
3. The day is recorded honestly enough that a second one can be designed
   against it rather than remembered.

The third condition is the one the first outing on 12 August could not meet.
Its account survives only as recollection.

## What would make this intent wrong

If the record grows faster than the teaching — if this house accumulates
governance about a workshop that did not help anybody — then the workshop was
the point and the house was overhead. Say so plainly and close it.
