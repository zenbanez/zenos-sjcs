# Governance

The Constitution governs each instantiated house. This RC0 repository is a
distribution candidate, not a central authority over receiving houses.

- A local steward decides whether to instantiate, adopt updates, or diverge.
- Upstream changes arrive as proposals and never overwrite local records.
- Constitutional substance changes through the local authority and
  deliberation rules in RFC-0006 and RFC-0007.
- The ZenOS name and conformance claims remain subject to a later adopted
  trademark and conformance policy; none is granted by this candidate.
- Publication, licence selection, and support obligations remain unadopted.
