# Bootstrap Workflow

1. A human accepts local stewardship.
2. The steward registers identity and author mapping.
3. The steward mints the founding intent and workspace.
4. The steward records a bounded delegation.
5. The participant emits a local root commission warrant.
6. The participant contributes on a branch and runs the local guard.
7. The steward reviews the diff and accepts, amends, or declines.

The optional scaffolder creates drafts only. Review and commit establish the
local institutional record.
