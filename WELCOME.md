# Welcome

You have arrived at an uninstantiated ZenOS base house.

You do not need to understand every RFC before beginning. Read in this order:

1. `constitution/RFC-0000-constitution-v2.md` — why the house exists.
2. `README.md` — what this candidate contains and excludes.
3. `ONBOARDING.md` — how a local steward creates the first real records.
4. `AGENTS.md` — the participation rules for humans and agents working here.

The seed has no local steward, participant, intent, evidence, or decision yet.
Those relationships come into being only through a local human steward's
explicit bootstrap. Templates are scaffolding, never lived records.

You may inspect everything without standing. To write, obtain a traceable local
intent and bounded delegation first.
